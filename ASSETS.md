## Used so far
No third-party art or audio is in the game yet. Everything you see and hear (soldiers, guns, buggies, bikes, weapon skins, weather, all sounds) is generated by our own code: boxes and cylinders in three.js, GLSL shaders, and ZzFX number-recipes. Libraries (three.js, Rapier, Yuka, Colyseus, zzfx) are MIT or similar and are listed in `docs/TECH_STACK.md`.
