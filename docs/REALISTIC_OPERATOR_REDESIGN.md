# NIGHTHAWK — Realistic Operator Redesign Brief

## Objective

Replace the early generic operator look with a coherent, realistic character-art pipeline. The aim is recognisable, believable people in practical tactical clothing, not exaggerated action figures or generic sci-fi soldiers.

## Approved style

- Photorealistic / realistic game-character presentation.
- Natural anatomy and individual facial identity.
- Plausible layered clothing and equipment with believable attachment and weight.
- PBR materials: skin, woven fabric, nylon webbing, polymer, painted metal, rubber, leather and dust.
- Cinematic menu lighting; performant gameplay assets.
- Muted Southern African dryland palette, with restrained per-character identifiers.

## Redesign principles

1. Start with likeness and silhouette, not armour.
2. Establish the character's personality and role with the owner before assigning equipment.
3. Build the clothing and gear around the approved role and environment.
4. Keep helmets, eyewear, scarves, carriers and packs modular.
5. Make the head readable both with and without headgear.
6. Avoid unsupported biographical claims and invented physical measurements.
7. Do not use real unit insignia, flags, brands or copyrighted logos without permission.
8. Use the generated concept art as mood-board material only; it is not a production model sheet.

## Character production stages

- Stage 1: reference board and likeness notes.
- Stage 2: neutral head studies and body silhouette.
- Stage 3: owner review of face and body proportions.
- Stage 4: uniform and modular gear design.
- Stage 5: front/side/back model sheet and material callouts.
- Stage 6: high-poly sculpt and retopology.
- Stage 7: UVs, PBR texture bake and hair solution.
- Stage 8: rig, animation tests and deformation review.
- Stage 9: GLB export, LODs and in-engine validation.

## Asset and performance targets

Initial targets, to be measured against the actual game:
- Hero operator: 1K–2K texture sets depending on screen prominence.
- Distant operators: lower-resolution LODs and reduced material count.
- Reuse body rigs and common gear materials where possible.
- Keep transparent hair/cloth cards limited.
- Use GLB/glTF and test loading, animation, draw calls, GPU memory and frame time in the browser.

## Approval required before implementation

The owner must approve:
- Face likeness.
- Body silhouette.
- Role and personality.
- Uniform silhouette and colour.
- Gear and weapon silhouette.
- Whether eyewear/headgear is permanent or optional.

No changes to gameplay operator definitions or abilities should be made from this art brief alone.
