# Licence audit — NIGHTHAWK local asset library

Generated 2026-11-19. Scope: the 30 pack folders on the shortlist plus the loose files in `raw-zips/`. Every licence/readme/credit/`.url`/PDF file inside those packs was located (see the end of this file) and the text of each was read. **Nothing was checked online** — web pages named below are pointers for manual verification, not evidence.

Status meanings: **VERIFIED** = the archive itself contains licence text naming the licence and giving commercial use; **CONDITIONAL** = explicit terms with restrictions that need a decision; **UNVERIFIED — DO NOT IMPORT** = no licence text in the archive, or only an informal sentence with no named licence, creator or source. Being inside a ZIP proves nothing about permission. This is an engineering record, not legal advice.

## Unresolved items that need a human decision

1. **URBAN, Utility Infrastructure, Shacks/Shanties/Sheds/Gantries, Street Furniture, Industrial exterior, tree_pack_1.1, PolyPack Vehicles-AI, Designersoup cars, PSX Style Cars (+ its 16 sounds), LowPolyGunAssets, RetroWeaponPack, the Wenrexa UI/icon packs and every loose file in `raw-zips/`**: no licence text exists in the archives. Find the original download page for each, record creator, URL, licence version and date in `ASSETS.md`, and keep a screenshot or saved copy of the licence. Until then they are not importable. These are the packs that would make the best map, first-person kit and HUD, so this is the highest-value paperwork.
2. **Human Basic Motions FREE** carries a "Standard Asset Store LA" notice (royalty-free, commercial use allowed, resale not allowed) and links to a licence page that was not read here. A marketplace end-user licence written for engine-bundled content may not cover serving raw animation files to browsers. Read `https://www.keviniglesias.com/license` and record a decision.
3. **CMU mocap (Anims_Only_glTF_V1)**: free for commercial products and for modification, but the terms say recipients of redistributed files must be told the same rules apply and the animations may not be resold, even converted. Decide whether a curated, retargeted handful of clips inside a game counts as acceptable. Not needed for the first slice.
4. **Ground087 and rocks_ground_03**: the project already lists the official ambientCG / Poly Haven pages as CC0 sources in `ASSETS.md`, but these ZIPs contain no licence file, so their origin is unproven. The clean fix is to re-download the 1K–2K sets from the official pages (needs your approval to download) and log the hashes.
5. **Quaternius "Standard" packs**: the archive text says CC0 for all five Quaternius packs on the list. `ASSETS.md` cautions that some newer Quaternius packs use a different licence, so confirm the download page says the same for these exact releases and note the date.
6. **Real-world designs and brands (independent of licence):** the Designersoup cars are recognisable look-alikes of a DeLorean, Land Rover Defender, Toyota Hilux, VW Beetle and a Mercedes-AMG GT; LowPolyGunAssets and the loose gun FBX files carry real firearm names (AK-47, AR-15, M16A1, M4A1, MP5, FN SCAR, AUG, FAMAS, L85A1, M1911). NIGHTHAWK's direction is fictional factions and fictional equipment, so rename and, where recognisable, redesign.
7. **Likeness/privacy (not an asset licence issue):** `main` records real photographs of friends as operator portraits "with permission". Keep them out of any shared asset catalogue and out of third-party tooling; this audit did not touch them.
