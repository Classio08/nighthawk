# Operation BLACK GLASS (planned, not built)

Fictional night infiltration. Original beats only. Not canon until the owner marks it so. Reuse the garrison alert ladder, disguise, and relay objectives that already exist. Do not add a new AI stack for this mission.

## Fantasy

KRG sold the unit's route. The buyer's archive is a glass server room under the industrial yard. The job is the drives, not the body count. Quiet is scored. Loud still extracts.

## Beats

1. Insert on foot, lights out. A stolen uniform lasts until a guard is close, a shot is fired, or the alert hits crisis.
2. Three terminals. Each one takes time. Starting a terminal raises "uneasy" if a guard's cone covers the room.
3. Optional: cut yard power. Searchlights die; hearing radius on guards grows.
4. Extract at the pad with the drives. If the alarm is crisis, VIPER can spawn using the same rules as Signal Blackout.

## Fail / success

- Success: drives in the extract zone, player alive.
- Fail: player dead, or the trace timer (reuse the Signal Blackout clock) hits zero before extract.

## Not in v1

New weapons, a vault minigame with its own physics, or dialogue trees. One radio line in, one debrief line out.
