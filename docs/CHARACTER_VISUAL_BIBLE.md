# NIGHTHAWK — Character Visual Bible

Status: first-pass art direction; gameplay implementation is intentionally untouched.

## Visual target

Build a grounded, recognisable fictional African special-operations unit identity: believable human proportions, readable silhouettes, practical kit, restrained olive/tan/charcoal palette, sun-worn materials, and strong face identity. Avoid generic sci-fi armour, oversized accessories, and excessive camouflage noise.

The owner selected a realistic redesign, confirmed the unit is fictional (not a real military or police unit), specified modern equipment, and supplied likeness references for Roms, Cleiseis, Shadow, Rampol and Maetja. Use photographs only as design references; do not reproduce social-media UI, captions, watermarks, or clothing logos.

## Character roster

The original eight are: Roms, Shadow, Douglas, Cliffhanger, Navy Blue, Rampol, Maetja, and Cleiseis.

Five characters have likeness references: Roms, Cleiseis, Shadow, Rampol and Maetja. Douglas, Cliffhanger and Navy Blue remain visually undefined until references or owner-approved descriptions are supplied. Do not invent their facial likenesses.

### Roms — reference batch 01

Reference: first uploaded Instagram screenshot (outdoor full-body photo).

Visible reference cues:
- Young adult man; medium/lean-looking build in the supplied photo.
- Short dark hair; sunglasses; moustache/goatee-style facial hair.
- Preserve the broad facial impression and recognisable head silhouette.
- The sports shirt and shorts are reference clothing only, not the final uniform.

Proposed game translation (editable design proposal):
- Role silhouette: adaptable assault / flex operator.
- Practical olive/earth-tone plate carrier, radio, magazine pouches, utility belt, gloves, knee protection and desert boots.
- Sunglasses can be an optional cosmetic; create a clear-eyed face version for close-ups and story scenes.
- No real-world brand logos.

### Cleiseis — reference batch 01

References: second uploaded screenshot (mirror/full-body selfie) and third uploaded portrait screenshot.

Visible reference cues:
- Short/buzzed hair.
- Distinctive face shape, brows, eyes and nose; use the portrait as the primary face reference.
- Athletic/average-looking build in the available photographs.
- Small ear jewellery is visible in the portrait; treat as optional cosmetic.
- The black T-shirt is reference clothing only, not the final uniform.

Proposed game translation (editable design proposal):
- Role silhouette: tactical support / control operator.
- Functional tan/olive carrier, radio, utility pouches, gloves, belt, knee protection and boots.
- Keep facial proportions and head silhouette recognisable under neutral lighting; helmet/headgear must be removable for portrait scenes.
- No real-world brand logos.

## Standard deliverables per operator

1. Neutral face portrait, front and 3/4 views.
2. Full-body front, side and back reference.
3. Headgear-off and headgear-on variants.
4. Base uniform plus one alternate palette.
5. Gear breakdown: vest, belt, backpack, gloves, boots, radio, pouches.
6. Facial expression set: neutral, focused, injured, radio-call.
7. Texture set: base colour, normal, roughness; optional AO/height where useful.
8. Game-ready GLB model, separate texture maps, source project, and a thumbnail.
9. LODs and texture budgets suitable for the browser game.

## Production rules

- Preserve likeness from supplied references without beautifying or changing identity-defining features.
- Do not infer height, exact weight, ethnicity, personality, military history, or biography from photographs.
- Any role, equipment, colour scheme or ability listed above is a proposal, not established canon.
- Keep face topology and hair/beard details readable at gameplay camera distance.
- Make gear modular so the same body rig can support different loadouts.
- Maintain consistent scale, rig naming, material naming and export settings across all eight operators.
- Obtain consent and ensure the project has rights to use reference photographs for likeness-based character creation and distribution.
